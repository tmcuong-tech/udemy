# C1186 Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) Training

Hands-on labs for learners preparing for the Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) exam and practical information systems audit work.

## Course Information

- **Course Code:** C1186
- **Course Title:** Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) Training
- **Duration:** 5 days / 35.5 hours
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Course Registration:** [Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) Training](https://www.tertiarycourses.com.sg/certified-information-systems-auditor-cisa-exam-prep.html)
- **Reference:** [CISA Complete Guide](https://certdemand.com/guides/cisa-complete-guide)

## Labs

| Domain | Lab | Title | Focus Area |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Information Systems Auditing Process | 01 | Audit Charter, Scope, and Risk-Based Planning | Audit objectives, scope, materiality, risk assessment, audit plan |
| Information Systems Auditing Process | 02 | Evidence Collection, Sampling, CAATs, Reporting, and Follow-Up | Evidence types, sampling, data analytics, findings, recommendations, follow-up |
| Governance and Management of IT | 03 | IT Governance, Strategy Alignment, and Policy Review | COBIT-style governance, roles, policy lifecycle, strategy alignment |
| Governance and Management of IT | 04 | Enterprise IT Risk, Portfolio, Resource, and Performance Audit | Risk registers, resource management, portfolio review, KPIs, quality assurance |
| IS Acquisition, Development, and Implementation | 05 | SDLC, Agile, DevSecOps, and Project Governance Audit | Requirements, feasibility, project controls, SDLC governance, change control |
| IS Acquisition, Development, and Implementation | 06 | Application Controls, Testing, Data Migration, and Post-Implementation Review | Input/process/output controls, UAT, migration, conversion, PIR |
| IS Operations and Business Resilience | 07 | IT Operations, Incident, Problem, Database, and Cloud Controls | ITSM, operations controls, incident/problem management, cloud and database controls |
| IS Operations and Business Resilience | 08 | Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Audit | BIA, RTO, RPO, recovery strategies, BCP/DRP testing |
| Protection of Information Assets | 09 | Access Control, IAM, Network Security, Encryption, and Data Protection | RBAC, MFA, least privilege, segmentation, PKI, privacy, classification |
| Protection of Information Assets | 10 | Vulnerability Management, Physical Security, Awareness, and CISA Readiness | Vulnerability lifecycle, pen test review, physical controls, social engineering, final capstone |

## Lab Requirements

- Laptop with spreadsheet and document editor
- PDF reader
- Browser access to the reference links
- Sample audit evidence supplied by instructor or created during labs
- Optional: spreadsheet templates for risk register, audit program, evidence matrix, and findings tracker
- Optional: access to a demo GRC, ticketing, IAM, SIEM, vulnerability, or cloud console environment
