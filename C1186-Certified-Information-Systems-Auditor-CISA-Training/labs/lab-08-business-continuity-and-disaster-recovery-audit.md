# Lab 08: Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Audit

## Domain

Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience

## Objective

Evaluate BIA, BCP, DRP, RTO, RPO, recovery strategy, backup, and testing evidence.

## Scenario

The organization claims its critical systems can be recovered within agreed timeframes. Audit must validate whether continuity and recovery plans are realistic, tested, and aligned to business needs.

## Tasks

1. Review a business impact analysis.
2. Identify critical business processes and supporting systems.
3. Record RTO and RPO for each critical system.
4. Compare RTO/RPO with backup and recovery capabilities.
5. Review recovery strategy and alternate processing arrangements.
6. Review BCP and DRP documents for ownership and update history.
7. Review test type, test scope, test results, and lessons learned.
8. Identify gaps between plan and evidence.
9. Draft a BCP/DRP audit conclusion.

## Deliverables

- BIA review sheet
- RTO/RPO matrix
- BCP/DRP checklist
- DR test evidence review
- Continuity audit finding

## Review Questions

1. Why does BIA come before recovery strategy?
2. How do RTO and RPO differ?
3. What makes a DR test meaningful?
4. What should audit do if recovery targets are not supported by evidence?
