# Lab 07: IT Operations, Incident, Problem, Database, and Cloud Controls

## Domain

Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience

## Objective

Audit operational controls for IT services, incidents, problems, databases, cloud environments, and infrastructure.

## Scenario

The operations team manages production systems across on-premises and cloud environments. Audit must assess whether operational processes are controlled, monitored, and resilient.

## Tasks

1. Review IT service management process documentation.
2. Test a sample of incidents for classification, escalation, resolution, and closure.
3. Test problem management records for root cause and prevention.
4. Review database administration controls such as privileged access, backup, and change logging.
5. Review cloud operations controls such as account ownership, monitoring, tagging, and configuration baselines.
6. Review SLA monitoring evidence.
7. Review job scheduling or batch process controls.
8. Identify operational control gaps.
9. Draft one operations audit finding.

## Deliverables

- Incident sample test sheet
- Problem management review
- Database controls checklist
- Cloud operations controls checklist
- Operations finding draft

## Review Questions

1. How do incident and problem management differ?
2. What evidence proves SLAs are monitored?
3. What database controls are commonly audited?
4. What cloud operations controls reduce governance risk?

Save the operations checklist and sample results.
