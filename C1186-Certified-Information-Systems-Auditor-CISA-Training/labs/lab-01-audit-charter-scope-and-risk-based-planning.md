# Lab 01: Audit Charter, Scope, and Risk-Based Planning

## Domain

Information Systems Auditing Process

## Objective

Create a risk-based audit plan for an information systems audit engagement.

## Scenario

Your audit team has been asked to review a customer registration platform that processes personal data and supports online payments. Management wants assurance over access, change management, data protection, and availability.

## Tasks

1. Draft the audit objective.
2. Define in-scope and out-of-scope systems, processes, teams, and time period.
3. Identify applicable criteria such as policy, regulation, contract, or framework requirements.
4. Identify key risks.
5. Rate each risk by likelihood and impact.
6. Define preliminary control objectives.
7. Prioritize audit areas based on risk.
8. Create an audit program outline with planned procedures.
9. Identify required skills, timeline, and stakeholders.

## Deliverables

- Audit charter summary
- Risk register
- Draft audit program
- Stakeholder interview list

## Review Questions

1. Why should audit scope be tied to risk?
2. What makes an audit objective testable?
3. How does materiality affect audit planning?
4. What should be excluded from scope and why?
