# Lab 03: IT Governance, Strategy Alignment, and Policy Review

## Domain

Governance and Management of IT

## Objective

Evaluate whether IT governance, strategy, roles, and policies support business objectives.

## Scenario

An organization has rapid technology growth but unclear ownership of IT decisions. The board wants assurance that IT investments, policies, and responsibilities align with business goals.

## Tasks

1. Identify key governance bodies and decision makers.
2. Map IT goals to business objectives.
3. Review roles and responsibilities for IT risk, security, operations, and audit.
4. Review the policy lifecycle from approval to communication and periodic review.
5. Identify missing or outdated policies.
6. Assess whether IT governance reporting is useful to management.
7. Draft audit questions for the CIO, CISO, and business owner.
8. Document gaps and recommendations.

## Deliverables

- Governance stakeholder map
- Strategy alignment matrix
- Policy review checklist
- Governance finding draft

## Review Questions

1. What evidence shows IT strategy alignment?
2. Why should policies have owners and review dates?
3. How does governance differ from management?
4. What should be reported to the board?

Save the governance matrix and policy checklist.
