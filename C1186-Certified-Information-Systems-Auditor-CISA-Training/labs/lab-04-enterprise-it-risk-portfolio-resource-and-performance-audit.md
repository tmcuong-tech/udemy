# Lab 04: Enterprise IT Risk, Portfolio, Resource, and Performance Audit

## Domain

Governance and Management of IT

## Objective

Assess IT risk management, portfolio oversight, resource planning, quality assurance, and performance monitoring.

## Scenario

The organization has many active IT projects and limited resources. Audit must determine whether risks, budgets, capacity, and performance are managed effectively.

## Tasks

1. Review a sample IT risk register.
2. Identify missing risk owners, ratings, responses, or review dates.
3. Review a sample IT project portfolio.
4. Assess whether projects align with strategic objectives.
5. Identify resource constraints and dependency risks.
6. Review quality assurance checkpoints.
7. Define KPIs and KRIs for IT governance reporting.
8. Draft a finding for one portfolio or resource management weakness.

## Deliverables

- IT risk register review
- Portfolio alignment matrix
- Resource and capacity risk notes
- KPI/KRI dashboard outline
- Audit finding draft

## Review Questions

1. What makes an IT risk register useful?
2. Why should projects be tied to business value?
3. How can resource constraints create control risk?
4. What is the difference between a KPI and a KRI?

## Cleanup

Save the portfolio and risk review notes.
