# Lab 05: SDLC, Agile, DevSecOps, and Project Governance Audit

## Domain

Information Systems Acquisition, Development, and Implementation

## Objective

Audit project governance and SDLC controls for a new information system.

## Scenario

A new customer portal was delivered using Agile and DevSecOps practices. Audit must review requirements, approvals, testing, security integration, change management, and project oversight.

## Tasks

1. Identify the project business case and success criteria.
2. Review requirements traceability.
3. Assess feasibility study and risk assessment evidence.
4. Review sprint, release, or milestone governance.
5. Identify security activities embedded into the lifecycle.
6. Review change approval and segregation of duties.
7. Review configuration management controls.
8. Identify missing evidence or weak controls.
9. Draft recommendations for stronger SDLC governance.

## Deliverables

- SDLC control checklist
- Requirements traceability review
- Change management test worksheet
- Project governance finding

## Review Questions

1. Why should auditors review requirements traceability?
2. How does Agile change the type of evidence available?
3. What is DevSecOps from an audit perspective?
4. Why is segregation of duties important in change management?
