# Lab 22 — Model Cards and Transparency

Create a SageMaker model card and complete each section, then distinguish model cards from Model Monitor and the Model Registry and examine AWS AI Service Cards.

**What you will do:**
- Create a SageMaker model card and declare intended use and explicit out-of-scope uses
- Assign a risk rating driven by consequence and autonomy, and justify it with evidence
- Record training provenance, time period, population and known gaps
- Record evaluation results disaggregated by facet group, plus caveats and limitations
- Distinguish model cards from the Model Registry and Model Monitor and explain how they interlock
- Examine AWS AI Service Cards and document the limitations you inherit from managed services
