# Lab 01 — AI, ML and Deep Learning Terminology

Build a precise vocabulary for AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI, and locate each concept in the AWS console.

**What you will do:**
- Survey the three layers of the AWS AI and ML stack in the console
- Place AI, ML, deep learning and generative AI in their correct nesting
- Classify workloads as supervised, unsupervised or reinforcement learning
- Separate the terms algorithm, training, model and inference
- Choose between real-time, batch, asynchronous and serverless inference
