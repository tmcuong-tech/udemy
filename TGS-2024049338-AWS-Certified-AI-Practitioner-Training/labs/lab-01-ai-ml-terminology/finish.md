**Key takeaways:**

- AI, ML, deep learning and generative AI are nested, not parallel — always choose the narrowest term that fits the scenario, and remember that a hand-written rule engine is AI but not ML.
- The learning paradigm is decided by the feedback signal: labels for supervised, none for unsupervised, a reward for reinforcement.
- An algorithm is a procedure, training produces a model, and inference uses that model — SageMaker gives each stage its own console page because each has its own cost profile.
- Real-time endpoints bill continuously and suit latency-sensitive requests; batch transform bills only for the job and suits large datasets where nobody is waiting.
- Parameters (weights and biases) are learned during training; hyperparameters (learning rate, layers, batch size) are set before it.
