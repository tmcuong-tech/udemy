# Lab 09 — Generative AI Capabilities and Limitations

Exercise what generative AI does well, then deliberately produce a hallucination, document it, and apply grounding and human review as mitigations.

**What you will do:**
- Exercise summarisation and generation on a realistic customer email
- Test translation and code generation, including whether exception instructions are honoured
- Deliberately provoke a hallucination and record the false claim verbatim with evidence
- Observe non-determinism, knowledge cutoff, bias and cost/latency limitations for yourself
- Re-run your hallucinated prompt with grounding, and match review models to risk levels
