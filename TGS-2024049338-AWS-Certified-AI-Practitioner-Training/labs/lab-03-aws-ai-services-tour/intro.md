# Lab 03 — AWS Managed AI Services Tour

Use Amazon Comprehend, Translate, Polly, Transcribe and Rekognition from the console, and learn to match each managed AI service to a business use case.

**What you will do:**
- Extract sentiment, entities, key phrases and PII from text with Amazon Comprehend
- Translate text and inspect round-trip quality with Amazon Translate
- Synthesise speech and control it with SSML in Amazon Polly
- Transcribe your own generated audio with Amazon Transcribe and evaluate the result
- Detect labels, moderate content and read text in images with Amazon Rekognition
- Match ten business requirements to the correct AWS AI service
