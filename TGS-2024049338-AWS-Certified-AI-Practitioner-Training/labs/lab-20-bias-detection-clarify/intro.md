# Lab 20 — Bias Detection with SageMaker Clarify

Frame a fairness question as facet, label and positive outcome, then compare pre-training and post-training bias metrics using either your own Clarify job or a guided bias report walkthrough.

**What you will do:**
- Define a fairness question precisely as facet, facet value, label and positive label value
- Explain the pre-training metrics Class Imbalance and Difference in Proportions of Labels
- Distinguish the post-training metric families and reason about error-rate gaps
- Either configure a Clarify bias job against your own SageMaker model, or
- Work through a guided bias report and identify inherited versus amplified bias
- Draft an issue report to the model owner stating observation, evidence, harm and recommendation
