# Lab 21 — Explainability and Feature Attribution

Use SHAP feature attribution in SageMaker Clarify to produce global and local explanations, then decide when a regulated use case requires an interpretable model instead.

**What you will do:**
- Distinguish global from local explanations and identify the audience each one serves
- Explain how SHAP attributes a prediction to features, and why the baseline defines what the explanation means
- Configure a Clarify explainability analysis, or follow the no-cost walkthrough
- Read a global and a local attribution report and write a plain-language refusal reason
- Weigh performance against interpretability and decide when the more accurate model must be rejected
