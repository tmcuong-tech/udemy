# Lab 11 — Amazon Q Business Getting Started

Create an Amazon Q Business application, connect it to documents in Amazon S3, and ask questions answered from those documents with citations.

**What you will do:**
- Understand what Amazon Q Business provides above Bedrock, and confirm its continuous cost
- Upload a small set of fictional company policy documents to Amazon S3
- Create a Q Business application with a service role and user identity configured
- Connect the S3 data source, run a sync, and interpret the sync history
- Ask questions answered from your documents and verify the citations
- Test where grounding stops — out-of-scope questions, false premises, stale documents and permissions

> **Cost warning:** Amazon Q Business is not free tier and bills for as long as the application exists. Complete this lab in one sitting and delete the application in Step 7.
