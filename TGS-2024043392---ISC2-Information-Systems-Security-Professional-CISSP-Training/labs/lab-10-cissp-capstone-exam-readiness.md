# Lab 10 - CISSP Capstone and Exam Readiness

## Objectives

- Complete an integrated CISSP security review.
- Produce management-ready recommendations.
- Identify weak domains.
- Build a personal exam study plan.
- Practice CISSP-style answer reasoning.

## Scenario

You are asked to brief the board on whether the customer portal is ready for production. You must summarize security posture, major risks, required controls, and residual risk.

## Steps

### 1. Consolidate Lab Evidence

Gather:

- Risk register.
- Asset classification table.
- Architecture diagram.
- Network diagram.
- IAM matrix.
- Assessment plan.
- Incident response plan.
- Secure SDLC checklist.

Use:

### 3. Create a Board Summary

Write:

```text
Current posture:
Top three risks:
Required decisions:
Residual risk:
Recommended next steps:
```

### 4. Practice Scenario Reasoning

For each practice question you miss, record:

```text
Question topic
My wrong assumption
Correct CISSP principle
Domain
What I will remember next time
```

### 5. Build a Study Plan

Create a 2-week plan:

1. Review weakest domains first.
2. Revisit practice questions by reasoning pattern.
3. Study control types and risk language daily.
4. Practice explaining answers aloud.
5. Review ethics and exam strategy before exam day.

### 6. Final Presentation

Present your recommendation in 5 minutes:

1. Business objective.
2. Key risks.
3. Control strategy.
4. Residual risk.
5. Go/no-go recommendation.

## Validation

You should have a domain confidence table, board summary, study plan, and exam mistakes log.

## Checkpoint Questions

1. Which domain is your weakest and why?
2. What is the most common reason you choose the wrong answer?
3. How do you identify the manager-level answer?
4. What must be true before production approval?

## Exam Focus

The CISSP exam rewards judgment. Read every question as a security leader: understand the business goal, protect life and legal obligations, manage risk, preserve evidence, and choose sustainable controls.
