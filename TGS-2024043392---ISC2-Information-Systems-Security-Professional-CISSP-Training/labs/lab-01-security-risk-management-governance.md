# Lab 01 - Security and Risk Management, Governance, Ethics

## Objectives

- Apply the security manager mindset.
- Build a risk register.
- Map policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines.
- Apply ethics and compliance reasoning.
- Choose risk treatment options.

## Scenario

Your organization is launching a customer portal. Management wants it live quickly, but the portal will store personal information and payment-related records. You must advise leadership before implementation begins.

## Steps

### 1. Identify Business Context

1. Write the business objective.
2. Identify key stakeholders.
3. Identify regulated or sensitive data.
4. Identify business impact if the portal is unavailable.
5. Record assumptions clearly.

### 2. Build a Risk Register

Create a table:

| Risk | Asset | Threat | Vulnerability | Impact | Likelihood | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unauthorized data access | Customer records | External attacker | Weak access controls | High | Medium | Mitigate |

### 3. Choose Risk Treatment

For each risk, choose one:

- Avoid.
- Transfer.
- Mitigate.
- Accept.

Write one sentence explaining why the selected option fits the business context.

### 4. Create a Policy Hierarchy

Draft:

1. Policy: high-level management intent.
2. Standard: mandatory minimum requirement.
3. Procedure: step-by-step implementation.
4. Guideline: recommended practice.

Example:

```text
Policy: Customer data must be protected according to legal and contractual requirements.
Standard: Administrative access requires MFA.
Procedure: Steps to enroll administrators into MFA.
Guideline: Use phishing-resistant MFA for privileged roles.
```

### 5. Apply Ethics

Answer:

1. What is your duty to protect society, the public good, and the organization?
2. What should you do if management asks you to skip security review?
3. What evidence should be recorded?

## Validation

You should have a risk register, policy hierarchy, and ethics decision note.

## Checkpoint Questions

1. Why does CISSP emphasize governance before tools?
2. What is the difference between risk appetite and risk tolerance?
3. Who should accept residual risk?
4. Why is written documentation important?

## Exam Focus

When in doubt, choose the answer that aligns security with business risk, ethics, governance, and formal decision-making.
